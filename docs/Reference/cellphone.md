# Chinese Mobile Number Guide

指南：办理并维护中国手机卡（外籍人士版）

---

## English Version | 英文版

### **I. Introduction**
In the current Chinese digital ecosystem, a local mobile number is essential for identity verification on major platforms such as **WeChat Pay** and **Alipay**. While these services are vital for daily transactions, they require "Real-Name Verification," which is most efficiently facilitated by a China-based mobile number.

### **II. Recommended Provider: China Unicom (中国联通)**
As of April 2026, [China Unicom](https://www.chinaunicom.com.cn/49/menu01/539/column06) is considered the most accessible carrier for foreign nationals. Their systems are well-equipped to handle passport-based applications, and the process typically concludes within 15–20 minutes.

### **III. The Application Process**
1.  **Documentation:** Present a valid **foreign passport**. Explicitly state that you are applying for a new line using a passport rather than a Chinese National ID.
2.  **Disclosure of Purpose:** When asked for the purpose of the line, clearly state that you are visiting friends/family and touring. This helps satisfy anti-fraud protocols.
3.  **Deposit:** A payment of **200 RMB** is generally sufficient to cover initial costs and credit.
4.  **Selecting a Plan:** Request a basic plan (e.g., the **39 RMB monthly plan**). Note that plan specifics (data vs. voice) vary by region.
5.  **Contractual Nuances:** Confirm there is **no long-term contract**. While staff may suggest you cannot change plans for 6 months, regulations typically allow modifications starting the next billing cycle (the 1st of the month).
6.  **Finalization:** The process concludes with a digital face recognition scan.

### **IV. Management & Maintenance**
* **App Login:** Download the [China Unicom App](http://u.10010.cn/qAaqC). Use your phone number and a Verification Code (SMS OTP) to log in.
* **The 8 RMB Plan:** To keep the number active long-term for a minimal fee, call **10010** (within China) or [+86 186-1861-0010](https://www.chinaunicom.com.cn/49/menu01/539/column06) (international) and request the **8 RMB monthly plan**.
* **International Roaming:** Ensure International SMS Roaming is active so you can receive verification codes (for apps like Douyin) while in the US or other countries.

## 中文版 | Chinese Version

### **一、 背景介绍**
在目前的中国数字化生态中，拥有一个本地手机号对于 **微信支付** 和 **支付宝** 的实名认证至关重要。虽然这些支付工具极大地方便了日常消费，但其身份验证环节通常需要一个中国手机号作为支撑。

### **二、 推荐运营商：中国联通 (China Unicom)**
截至 2026 年 4 月，[中国联通](https://www.chinaunicom.com.cn/49/menu01/539/column06) 对持外国护照办理业务的访客最为友好。其办理流程非常规范，通常在 15-20 分钟内即可完成。

### **三、 办理流程及建议**
1.  **所需证件：** 携带有效的 **外国护照**。办理时请明确告知工作人员需使用护照开户（非二代身份证）。
2.  **开卡用途：** 工作人员会询问电话用途以防范电信诈骗。请如实回答为“来华探亲及旅游”。
3.  **预存话费：** 通常预存 **200 元人民币** 即可满足开户要求。
4.  **选择套餐：** 建议先办理 **39 元月租套餐**。
    * *注：* 不同城市的套餐内容有所差异（例如深圳的套餐可能偏重流量，包含 20GB 流量但不含语音，通话按 0.15 元/分钟计费）。
5.  **避坑指南：** * 确认该套餐 **无合约 (No Contract)**。
    * 工作人员可能会声称 6 个月内不可更改套餐，这通常并非真实规定。实际上，用户可以在下个月初（新的账单周期）更改套餐。
6.  **完成办理：** 系统录入后需进行人脸识别，随后即可激活。

### **四、 数字化管理与长期维护**
* **App 登录：** 下载 [中国联通 APP](http://u.10010.cn/qAaqC)。中国主流的登录方式为“手机号+验证码”，无需设置复杂密码。
* **保号方案（8 元套餐）：** 若想长期保留此号码以接收验证码，请拨打客服热线 **10010**（国内）或 [+86 186-1861-0010](https://www.chinaunicom.com.cn/49/menu01/539/column06)（国际免费专线），要求更改为 **8 元低功耗套餐**。
* **国际漫游：** 确保开启国际短信漫游功能。这样即使回到美国，也能正常接收抖音、银行等应用的验证码短信。
