Last week the air conditioning stopped working on my 2015 Lexus RX350. The
blower still worked, but the system would not cool. My first checks were the
simple electrical ones: relay and fuse. I could not find any obvious issue
there, so I kept looking.

<!-- truncate -->

The useful clue came from this ClubLexus thread:
[RX350 2010/2011 Air conditioning DTC B1479 cheap solution](https://www.clublexus.com/forums/rx-3rd-gen-2010-2015/824215-rx350-2010-2011-air-conditioning-dtc-b1479-cheap-solution.html).
The thread describes an A/C flow sensor problem where the compressor may not
engage because the control system sees an out-of-range sensor signal. The
official fix often points toward replacing more expensive A/C components, but
people in the thread had success using a small magnet near the sensor to bring
the signal back into a usable range.

I bought a replacement sensor, but the sensor location is extremely hard to
reach on the compressor. After fighting with the access, I tried the magnet
trick instead. With the magnet in place, the compressor started running again.
That confirmed I was likely dealing with the same sensor-related issue instead
of a bad relay or fuse.

I also adjusted the refrigerant side. I released a small amount of refrigerant,
then used a new can of R-134a to refill the system to the right pressure. After
that, the A/C started cooling again.

## Current status

- Relay and fuse checked with no obvious fault found.
- Replacement sensor purchased, but not installed because access is very tight.
- Magnet workaround tested and compressor started running again.
- R-134a refrigerant refilled to the target pressure.
- A/C is cooling again.
