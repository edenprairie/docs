Today I refreshed this personal document site so it feels less like a default
docs template and more like a working archive.

<!-- truncate -->

The biggest change is the structure. The site now has a clearer split between
work notes, personal material, home and family references, reusable guides, and
dated blog entries. The goal is simple: when I add a document later, I should
know where it belongs without reorganizing the entire site again.

I also brought the blog back as a place for short dated notes. Documents are for
material I expect to maintain. Blog posts are for updates, reflections, and
context that belongs to a specific day.
